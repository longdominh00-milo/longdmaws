Day 1 - Monitoring and Troubleshooting
- Used grep and find to investigate application logs.
- Practiced top, vm_stat, sysctl vm.swapusage, and df -h on macOS.
- Low free RAM does not automatically mean severe memory pressure.
- Swap usage is a current value; swapins/swapouts are cumulative counters.
- Compare metrics over time and correlate with application performance.
- Troubleshooting workflow: Observe -> Correlate -> Investigate -> Act -> Verify.
- Never restart or resize a server based on one metric alone.
